Discover the island of Sumba

Sumba sits at the southern edge of Indonesia, where the Antarctic current keeps the air moving and the temperature between 21 and 33 degrees all year. Sunny days are the norm here and the night sky is a nightly event.

Nature still sets the rhythm: empty beaches, wild savannah, waterfalls cut into the rock, and villages that have kept the Marapu beliefs and their ancestral architecture for more than a thousand years. Indonesia is one of the world's surf capitals, and Sumba has the waves without the crowd.

NIHI Sumba, Lelewatu, Umarato, Bobocabin and Hotel Sanubari are all on the island: luxury arrived here before mass tourism did.
