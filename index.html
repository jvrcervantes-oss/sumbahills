Discover the island of Sumba

Sumba Hills sits in Sumba, an authentic island of the Indonesian archipelago where nature remains untouched and time seems to stand still.

In Indonesia's southernmost reach, Sumba is cooled by the Antarctic current — an outdoor climate all year round, with lows of 21º and highs of 33º. Sunny days are the norm and starlit nights an everyday spectacle.

World-class nature is on your doorstep: ancestral waterfalls, endless beaches and villages that still keep millenary traditions — alongside beach clubs and resorts such as the award-winning NIHI Sumba, Lelewatu, Umarato and Sanubari.
