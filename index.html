The island

An island still writing its first chapter

Sumba sits at the southern edge of Indonesia, where the Antarctic current keeps the air moving and the temperature between 21 and 33 degrees all year. Sunny days are the norm here and the night sky is a nightly event.

Nature still sets the rhythm: empty beaches, wild savannah, waterfalls cut into the rock, and villages that have kept the Marapu beliefs and their ancestral architecture for more than a thousand years. Indonesia is one of the world's surf capitals, and Sumba has the waves without the crowd. NIHI Sumba, Lelewatu, Umarato, Bobocabin and Hotel Sanubari are all on the island: luxury arrived here before mass tourism did.

Connections

Remote, not cut off

One hour's flight from Bali, or from Darwin in Australia. From the island's main airport it is another hour to the gate: close enough to reach in a morning, far enough that nobody arrives by accident.

The name

Sandalwood Island

Sumba was once Pulau Cendana, the Sandalwood Island, connected to the world through the trade of one of Asia's most valuable woods. Sandalwood meant more than money here. It carried spiritual weight and a link to the ancestors in the rituals of the Marapu tradition.

The Sandalwood pony became the island's other emblem, a symbol of status and spiritual connection present in ceremony, in ikat weaving and in the island's own name. The resort takes its name from both.

Ownership

How a foreigner holds land in Indonesia

Indonesian law does not give a foreign national the same title as an Indonesian citizen. There are three routes, and the one that applies depends on who is buying. All of them are formalised before an authorised notary.

Foreign individual

Up to 90 years

A right of use (Hak Sewa), structured as three successive contracts of up to 30 years each.

Company in Indonesia · PT PMA

Up to 80 years

A building-use right (Hak Guna Bangunan, HGB) acquired through an Indonesian company. This route allows the full transfer of the right from the previous owner to the company.

Indonesian national

Full ownership

Transfer of the ownership right (Hak Milik, SHM), formalised by public deed before an authorised notary.

Which route fits your case is set out in writing, with the title documents, before any payment is made. This page is a summary and not legal advice.

The process

Payments tied to what is built

Nothing is paid against a promise. Each release is matched to a stage of the work that can be checked on site.
